Quick answers.

Everything you might ask before launch day.

When does LAVA launch?

LAVA is planned to launch on 20 November 2026. The live countdown at the top of this page counts down to that date.

What is LAVA?

LAVA is a voice-first assistant concept: you talk, it listens, thinks and answers. This page shows a preview of that experience.

Is the voice demo real AI?

Right now the demo runs in sample mode: it knows a few topics and replies from inside this page, with nothing sent to a server. Open-ended AI answers need a secure backend, which is the next step.

Which devices and browsers work?

The page is built for phones and laptops. Voice input works best in recent Chrome or Edge; if your browser doesn’t support it, you can type to LAVA instead.

How do I book a seat?

Tap “Book your seat on WhatsApp” in the Book section. A ready-to-send message opens in WhatsApp; send it to confirm your seat.

What happens to my details and voice?

When you book on WhatsApp, only the message you choose to send reaches us, through WhatsApp. For voice, your browser’s speech recognition handles the audio, and it may be processed by the browser’s own speech service.
